I am a fourth-year CS Ph.D. student at [Rutgers University](https://www.rutgers.edu/), working with Professor [Dimitris N. Metaxas](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=a7VNhCIAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao). I received my B.E. in Software Engineering from [Nanjing University](https://www.nju.edu.cn/).

My recent work includes:

- **LLM/VLM Agents**: building LLM/VLM agents that leverage agentic capabilities to solve real-world tasks, including GUI agents [[R-WoM](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2510.11892)] and agentic memory [[A-MEM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12110)].
- **Infrastructure for Agent Serving**: exploring infrastructure for efficiently serving agents, including agentic OS [[AIOS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.16971)], semantic filesystem [[LSFS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.11843)], and model routing [[OmniRouter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.20576)].
